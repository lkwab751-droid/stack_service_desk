Service desk, stack version

New customers are placed on top of a pile, and the customer on top is called first (last in, first out). Priority customers are always called before regular ones. Each counter only serves the services it is staffed for.

    What changes with a stack

    In the queue version, the customer who has waited longest is called first, so everyone gets a fair turn. With a stack the newest customer is called first, so someone who arrived early can be left at the bottom of the pile for a very long time. Add a batch of random customers, call a few, and watch the longest wait grow. That is why real service desks use queues, and why stacks suit undo, back buttons and similar "most recent first" jobs.
